PRIVATE YOGA SESSIONS

TAILOR-MADE FOR YOU

I offer private 1:1 yoga lessons around Gower and Swansea, individually tailored to your goals. Whether you are brand new to yoga or an advanced practitioner, private lessons can help you deepen your physical and spiritual practice and learn more about yoga… and about yourself!

BENEFITS OF PRIVATE LESSONS

  • Personalised yoga flow sequencing
  • Personalised instructions and explanations
  • Questions can be asked throughout class
  • Personalised guided meditations
  • A class pace that works for your body
  • An experienced teacher’s undivided attention
  • Comfort and privacy
  • Flexible scheduling

I offer a complimentary sound bath with voice and lyre to help you relax into the final moments of my private classes. And to anyone interested in booking, I offer a free online consultation in advance of our first session, to discuss your goals, individual needs, and any conditions or injuries you may be working with.
