SEASONAL NEWSLETTER

Have you ever experienced the real magic of presence? You might think of it as mindfulness, or flow state, or simply as truly paying attention. Whatever you prefer to call it, that magic is the focus of my yoga classes and my art. At the start of each season I share an email newsletter broadly themed around presence, with insight into yogic philosophy, seasonal journal prompts, and some of my nature photography, as well as updates about my upcoming yoga events and offers. It’s a love letter to mindfulness (in meditation, in art, and in life) and I love writing it! My hope is to shine light on moments which can often be overlooked — from a muscular microadjustment, to raindrops sparkling on a spider's web — that transform the everyday into the extraordinary and connect us to wonder, to the universe, and to ourselves.
