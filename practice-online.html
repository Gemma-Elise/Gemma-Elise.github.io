PRACTICE ONLINE

Joining group yoga classes online allows us the comfort of practicing from our own homes, and can be more convenient as well as cheaper than practicing in-studio. My online group classes are designed specifically for an online meeting — they are not pre-recorded or exactly the same as an in-person class, but thoughtfully planned to help you have the best experience through Zoom.

I design my online classes with care, including:

  • Precise verbal cueing so you don’t need to keep looking at your screen
  • A sequence that builds gradually, using rhythm and repetition to help you feel into the flow
  • Clear visualisations to help you understand and adjust your alignment
  • Carefully curated music (that you can actually hear!) to set the mood
  • The option to turn your mic on if you have a question – your voice is valued!

SLOW VINYASA FLOW

A slow but playful flow with creative sequencing and thoughtful transitions; an invitation into intentional movement and embodied awareness.

HATHA ALCHEMY

Slow-burn magic; a chance to experience the alchemy of holding poses for longer, and a deeper dive into pranayama (yogic breathing techniques).

WEEKLY SANGHA

The Sanskrit word ‘Sangha’ means community; a space to come together to practice with like-minded people, which can help us strengthen our sense of purpose as well as our sense of belonging. This class is a little different to the others on my schedule because it is inspired by my real morning practice and is more of a mixture of styles, drawing inspiration from hatha, vinyasa, kriya, kundalini, and qigong.
