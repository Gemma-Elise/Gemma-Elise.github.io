MEDITATION MENTORSHIP

MEDITATION MADE SIMPLE

Have you ever wished you could learn how to meditate? Many of us feel called towards meditation but find it difficult to settle into a regular practice. We carve out time from our busy days to sit down, we close our eyes, and we find ourselves… distracted by anything and everything. If you have had an experience like this, you are not alone!

My life changed forever when I was shown how to meditate in a way that actually worked for me, and I love passing these lessons on. I offer private meditation classes, both online and in-person, which include:

  • Clear guidance
  • Personalised advice for your needs
  • Warm up exercises tailor-made for your body
  • Breathing techniques to help settle in to the practice
  • Different meditation techniques to find what works best for you
  • Clear and simple explanation of all techniques
  • Personalised visualisations
  • Space for any questions you might have after each session
  • Journal prompts for collecting your thoughts after each session
